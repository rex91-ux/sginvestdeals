Fees are read from each platform’s own pricing page on 24 Oct 2026. POEMS and Syfe SGX rates step down as your account grows (POEMS 0.08% → 0.07% from S$30k; Syfe 0.06% → 0.055% from S$50k); the calculator assumes your account is at least the trade size. moomoo is shown at its standard fees; new users pay no SGX commission for their first year (shown alongside). Endowus and Syfe charge the rate of the tier your total falls into; StashAway charges each slice at its own rate, and AutoWealth adds a flat US$18 (about S$24) to 0.5%, which is why the cheapest robo changes with size.
