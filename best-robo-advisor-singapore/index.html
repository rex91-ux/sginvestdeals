Why the winner changes

Endowus charges 0.60% with no flat fee, so it’s cheapest on small sums. AutoWealth charges a flat US$18 plus just 0.5%, so once your portfolio passes about S$15k the low percentage wins. Syfe drops to 0.45% on the whole balance from S$250k. StashAway charges each slice at a falling rate, so it only catches up on the largest portfolios — from about S$620k.

Questions

Frequently asked

Which is the cheapest robo-advisor in Singapore?

It depends on the amount. By platform fee on 24 Oct 2026: Endowus below about S$15,000, AutoWealth from about S$15,000 to S$250,000, Syfe from S$250,000 to about S$620,000, and StashAway above that. On S$100,000 that is S$524 a year at AutoWealth against S$600 at Syfe, S$654 at Endowus and S$736 at StashAway.

Which robo-advisor can invest my CPF?

Endowus invests CPF OA, CPF SA and SRS at 0.40% a year plus GST. AutoWealth invests CPF OA at 0.28%. Syfe and StashAway accept SRS but not CPF.

Do these fees include fund costs?

No. Each platform also has fund-level costs (fund expense ratios) that differ between them. The comparison here covers the platform fee each one charges.

Is AutoWealth’s fee before or after GST?

AutoWealth quotes 0.5% a year plus a US$18 platform fee and doesn’t mention GST; its own example puts S$10,000 at about S$74 a year. The figures here follow AutoWealth’s quote.

Which robo-advisor has the best sign-up reward?

Syfe’s is the largest: 6 to 12 months of management fees waived, or S$20–450 of brokerage credit. StashAway gives an extra 3 months without fees; Endowus and AutoWealth each give S$20.
