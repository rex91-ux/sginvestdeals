Full details

The referral reward in full

Tiger’s invite page shows USD 220: a USD 10 voucher when you first deposit at least USD 10, plus USD 7 a day for 30 days (USD 210) if you deposit at least USD 1,000 and keep it there. Tiger’s campaign terms set the daily amount by the referrer’s membership level — USD 6 a day (USD 180) for some referrers — so treat USD 220 as the ceiling.

The vouchers offset the cost of an order (stocks, options, funds; not fractional shares) and last 90 days after activation. Deposits into Tiger’s Cash Boost account don’t count. To withdraw any proceeds made using reward vouchers you need at least 10 trades of USD 3 or more. Campaign period: 29 Sep – 30 Oct 2026, 3pm.

Tiger’s standard welcome gifts (stack with the invite)

On top of the invite reward, Tiger’s regular welcome tiers pay a US commission card plus stock cash coupons on net deposits within 30 days of your first deposit: S$100 + S$100 above S$3,000, S$200 + S$200 above S$10,000, and S$500 + S$500 above S$100,000. The coupon half needs the deposit kept for 60 days. That’s the “up to S$1,000”.

Every fee, line by line

US: commission and platform fee are each capped at 0.5% of the trade; a third-party settlement fee of US$0.003/share applies; sells also pay the SEC fee and FINRA TAF. Fractional orders under one share pay no commission but a 1% platform fee (max US$1).

SGX: plus SGX trading fee 0.0075% and clearing fee 0.0325%. Custody fee S$2/quarter is currently waived. GST: 9% applies to Tiger’s fees.

Questions

Frequently asked

How much is the Tiger Brokers referral reward?

Up to USD 220 in trade vouchers on Tiger’s invite page: USD 10 when you first deposit, plus USD 7 a day for 30 days if you keep at least USD 1,000 deposited. Tiger’s terms pay some referees USD 6 a day instead, depending on the referrer’s level. Tiger’s standard welcome gifts, up to S$1,000 in commission cards and stock coupons, stack on top.

Is Tiger cheaper than POEMS or Syfe?

Not on fees. On US stocks POEMS and Syfe charge no commission. On SGX, Tiger and Syfe cost the same on most trade sizes (S$109 on S$100,000 against POEMS’s S$120), and Syfe gets cheaper once you hold S$50,000 with it. POEMS is cheapest on SGX trades under about S$2,000. Tiger’s advantage is its sign-up reward.

Are the Tiger rewards cash?

No. They are vouchers that offset the cost of an order, valid for 90 days. Gains made using them can be withdrawn only after at least 10 trades.

When does the Tiger referral campaign end?

The current campaign runs to 30 October 2026, 3pm Singapore time. Tiger usually replaces it with a new one; this page is updated when it does.
