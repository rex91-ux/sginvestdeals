Questions

Frequently asked

What does the Endowus promo code give?

A S$20 fee credit for new users who sign up through the invite link and invest at least S$1,000 from cash, CPF or SRS.

How much does Endowus charge?

0.60% a year on cash portfolios below S$200,000 and 0.50% from S$200,000 to S$1 million, plus GST. CPF and SRS portfolios are 0.40% flat (0.30% for a single fund). Fund-level fees of 0.05% to 1% are charged on top.

Is Endowus cheaper than AutoWealth, Syfe or StashAway?

Below about S$15,000, yes — S$65 a year on S$10,000 against S$71 at Syfe, S$74 at AutoWealth and S$87 at StashAway. Above that AutoWealth’s flat 0.5% + US$18 is lower: S$524 against S$654 at Endowus on S$100,000, and Syfe and StashAway take over on bigger portfolios. Fund costs, which differ between them, are not included.

Can I invest CPF with Endowus?

Yes — CPF OA, CPF SA and SRS, at 0.40% a year for multi-fund portfolios. AutoWealth also invests CPF OA, for 0.28%; StashAway and Syfe accept SRS but don’t offer CPF investing.
