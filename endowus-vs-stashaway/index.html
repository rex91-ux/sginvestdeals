Endowus vs StashAway

Endowus is cheaper up to about S$425k. StashAway wins above that.

On S$100k, Endowus costs S$654 a year against StashAway’s S$736. Endowus also takes CPF; StashAway doesn’t. StashAway’s tiered slices only pull ahead on large portfolios.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Endowus or StashAway cheaper?

Endowus, for cash portfolios up to about S$425,000 — S$654 a year on S$100,000 against S$736 at StashAway, incl. GST (24 Oct 2026). Above about S$425,000 StashAway’s sliced fees come out lower: S$2,643 against S$2,725 on S$500,000. Fund costs are extra for both.

Can I invest CPF with StashAway?

No. StashAway accepts SRS but doesn’t offer CPF investing. Endowus invests CPF OA, CPF SA and SRS at 0.40% a year plus GST.

What is the minimum to start?

Endowus needs S$1,000. StashAway has no minimum.

Which has the better sign-up reward?

StashAway’s invite link gives an extra 3 months without management fees, worth about S$184 on S$100,000. Endowus’s code KVXXK gives a S$20 fee credit once you invest S$1,000.

Are there cheaper robo-advisors than both?

By platform fee, yes, from about S$15,000 up: AutoWealth (0.5% + US$18 a year) is cheapest from about S$15k to S$250k, and Syfe from S$250k to about S$620k. Endowus is cheapest below about S$15k.
