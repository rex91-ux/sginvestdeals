StashAway vs Endowus vs Syfe at three sizes

Platform fee a year on a cash portfolio, incl. 9% GST: S$10k — StashAway S$87, Endowus S$65, Syfe S$71. S$100k — StashAway S$736, Endowus S$654, Syfe S$600. Because StashAway’s slices keep falling, it is the cheapest of the three from about S$620k to S$1M, and again above about S$1.3M (between those, Syfe’s 0.35% tier briefly wins).

The referral and welcome rewards

Through an invite, you get an extra 3 months’ management-fee waiver if you sign up, finish onboarding and deposit into an eligible portfolio within 30 days. StashAway’s separate welcome rewards (8 – 30 Oct 2026), advertised as worth up to S$1,670, include a S$50 ETF rebate when you invest through ETF Explorer and a return booster of up to 0.8% a year for 3 months on General Investing, Singapore Investing and Thematic portfolios.

Other fees

StashAway Simple (cash) 0.15% a year and Simple Plus 0.2%, both outside the tiers. A single-ETF Flexible Portfolio is a flat 0.3%. ETF Explorer charges US$1 per ETF buy or sell. Fees are deducted monthly; ETF expense ratios are inside the fund prices.

Questions

Frequently asked

What do I get with the StashAway invite?

An extra 3 months without management fees, if you sign up, complete onboarding and deposit into an eligible portfolio within 30 days. StashAway’s welcome rewards run separately.

How much does StashAway charge?

0.8% a year on the first S$25,000, falling slice by slice to 0.2% above S$1 million, before GST. ETF expense ratios are extra.

Is StashAway cheaper than Endowus or Syfe?

On large portfolios, yes — it is the cheapest of the three from about S$620,000. Below that it isn’t: on S$100,000 StashAway is about S$736 a year against S$600 at Syfe and S$654 at Endowus, and on S$500,000 it is S$2,643 against Syfe’s S$2,453.

Can I invest CPF with StashAway?

No. StashAway accepts SRS money but not CPF. Endowus takes both.
